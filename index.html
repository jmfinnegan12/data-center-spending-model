How much will US data center construction spending be in October 2026?

Question Model

GitHub · suggest a change · how to contribute

The number

Each month the Census Bureau estimates the value of private data center construction put in place in the US, as a seasonally adjusted annual rate.1 Buildings, site work, backup power and cooling count; racks and servers do not.2 The monthly series starts in January 2014.1

How Census estimates it

  • A sample of projects. Drawn from Dodge Construction Network's lists of project starts. Projects of $100 million or more are all included.3
  • Owners report monthly, voluntarily. For a project that has not reported, Census fills in its total cost times the share that similar projects had completed at the same age. In 2025, 78% of private nonresidential dollars were filled in this way.3
  • Earlier months change. Filled-in values are redone each month and late reports replace them, so each release revises the two months before it. A yearly revision comes each July.3,4
  • Projects missing from the lists are covered by raising the total 25%.3

The question

Will the first published figure for October 2026 be above each of eleven strikes, from $90B to $115B in steps of $2.5B? Census publishes it on Dec 1, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. ET.5 Later revisions do not count.6 Rules →
