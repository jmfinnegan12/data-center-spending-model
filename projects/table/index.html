Capacity is as each source states it: IT load for some projects, total facility power for others, which runs higher. Owner: who owns the buildings. Builder: general contractor. Customer: who uses the capacity. Confirmed: stated by the owner, builder, customer or a government record. Estimated: a third party. Census counts buildings, power and cooling, not chips and servers, so an investment figure that includes them overstates construction. Latest news: ↑ expanding or ahead, ↓ cut or delayed, ↕ mixed.
